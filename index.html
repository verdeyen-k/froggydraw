        How to play

        Everyone copies the same picture as pixel art. The closer your copy, the more points you get!

        🐸 Getting started

        • Type your name and tap Create a room. You get a 4-letter code.
        • Friends open the game on their own phones or computers, type the code and tap Join. 2 to 8 players.
        • Everyone picks a frog. Each frog is a different color and mood:
        • While you wait, chat! Tap a frog face under the chat to put it in your message.
        • The host picks how many rounds, how long each round is, and whether to play 🌈 Color Craze, then taps Start.

        ✏️ Drawing

        • The picture to copy is in the middle of the board. Everyone else's drawing shows around it as they draw.
        • Press and hold the middle picture to see it bigger.
        • Your grid is under the board. The darker lines every 4 squares help you count where things go.
        • Tap a color, then tap or drag on your grid. 🪣 Fill colors a whole area at once, and the 🧽 square erases.
        • ↩️ Undo takes back your last move. 🗑️ Clear starts over (and Undo brings it back).
        • Tap ✅ Done when you're finished. The round ends when everyone is done or the timer runs out.

        ⭐ Points

        • For every square of the picture: the right color in the right spot gets 1 point, and any other color there gets ½.
        • Coloring squares that should be empty costs you, so don't scribble!
        • Your score for the round is how much you got right, out of 100.
        • After the last round, the frog with the most points wins. 🏆

        🌈 Color Craze

        Every color in the picture swaps for a different one, so the frog might be purple with orange eyes. Copy the crazy colors you see, not the normal ones!

        🎨 Practice alone

        Pick any picture (or 🎲 Surprise me), copy it with no timer, and tap Check to see your score.
